# Product

## What it is
Mieux is a grocery decision tool for French shoppers. Scan a barcode in the supermarket and it answers: is this worth its price, and what should I buy instead? Web app (installable PWA) is the product today; native mobile is planned, not built.

## Users and moment
Someone standing in a supermarket aisle, phone in one hand, a few seconds to decide. Mostly French-speaking, price-conscious, health-aware but not willing to fill in a profile or follow a diet.

## Promise
- A score out of 100, the price per kilo, and three alternatives from the same aisle: cheapest, best quality, best value.
- Unknown price is said to be unknown, never invented. Prices come from Open Prices and the user's own receipts; product data from Open Food Facts.
- A declared allergen removes the product.

## Positioning
Scanners like Yuka score the product. Mieux scores it and puts the price per kilo and the alternatives next to the score.

## Tone
Plain, calm, honest. French first (English available). No hype, no health claims beyond the data, no guilt.

## Principles
1. One message and one action per screen.
2. Show the number a shopper wants first: the price difference per kilo.
3. Never fabricate data; show provenance and date.
4. Works in seconds, one-handed, without an account or install.
5. Respect privacy: scans stay on the device, audience measurement is aggregate and cookie-free.

## Surfaces
- Landing (Persuade), App screens: scan, product, alternatives, compare, list, history, profile (Operate), Blog (Read).

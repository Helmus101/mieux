# Mieux: scan it, know if it's worth it

## hook
You are in a supermarket aisle, holding a product. [#hold] Is it worth its price? [#q] And what could you buy instead? [#alt]

## scan
Meet {Mieux|myoo}. [#name] Scan the barcode, and you get a score out of one hundred, [#score] and the price per kilo. [#price]

## alternatives
Then it shows three alternatives from the same shelf. [#cards] The cheapest, [#c1] the best quality, [#c2] and the best value. [#c3] On a real chocolate bar, the cheapest costs {39|thirty-nine} percent less per kilo. [#less]

## honest
Prices come from Open Prices and from real receipts. [#src] When a price is unknown, {Mieux|myoo} says so, [#unknown] and never invents one. [#never]

## privacy
Your scans stay on your phone, [#phone] and it is free, with nothing to install. [#free]

## outro
If you like {Mieux|myoo}, watch, like and share this video, [#ask] and help us win the Audience Vote. [#vote]
